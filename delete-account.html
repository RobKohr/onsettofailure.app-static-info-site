Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to terminate your account and permanently delete all associated fitness, health, and profile data from our servers, you may submit a request using the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion

To request permanent account deletion, send an email to our support contact from the email address registered with your One Set To Failure account.

Requirements & Timeline

  • Subject Line: Please use Account Deletion Request as your email subject line.
  • Email Verification: The request must originate from the email address associated with your active account to verify account ownership.
  • Processing Window: Account deletion requests are processed and completed within 30 days of receipt.

What Data is Deleted

Upon receipt of your request, the following data is permanently purged from our servers:

  • Your account credentials and email profile
  • All logged workout records, 1RM history, and set details
  • All tracked health and body composition data (weight, body fat %, calorie targets)
  • All customized exercise and recovery matrix settings

Privacy Policy

App: One Set to Failure • Effective Date: August 11, 2026 • Last Updated: August 11, 2026

Neverall Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the One Set to Failure mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App and related services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us when you register, use the App, or communicate with us.

A. Personal Identification Data

  • Email Address: Used as your primary identifier for authentication via one-time passcodes (OTP) and for account recovery.

B. Health, Fitness & Biometric Metrics

To provide personalized strength progression calculations, fitness tracking, and performance analytics, we collect and store:

  • Physical characteristics (Height, Weight, Age)
  • Body composition metrics (Estimated Body Fat Percentage)
  • Nutritional data (Caloric intake logs)
  • Exercise and performance data (Workout logs, exercise history, target reps, weight lifted, and calculated One-Rep Max metrics)

C. Financial & Billing Information

Payment processing for trial conversions, monthly subscriptions, or lifetime memberships is handled directly by third-party app stores (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store). We do not collect or store full credit card numbers or financial account details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to operate, maintain, and improve the App:

  • Authentication: To log you in securely using email-based one-time codes without storing passwords.
  • Core Functionality: To calculate progression metrics (including back-calculated 1RMs), track historical trends, and sync your fitness data across your devices.
  • Account Management: To manage subscriptions, account status, and free trial periods.
  • Communication: To send transactional emails (login passcodes, critical service updates). If you explicitly opt in, we may send health/fitness notifications or app update emails. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

3. Data Storage & Hosting Infrastructure

  • Backend Services: Your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS and stored in cloud database server infrastructure provided by our hosting partners (currently Linode / Akamai Technologies, or equivalent cloud server providers).
  • Data Security: Data in transit is strictly encrypted using standard SSL/HTTPS protocols. Data stored in our database is protected via system access controls and network isolation.

4. Analytics, Tracking & Third Parties

  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics, PostHog, or Mixpanel) to track your in-app behavior.
  • No Telemetry / Crash Reporting: We do not run background crash reporting tools or telemetry agents (such as Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry).
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal, fitness, or biometric data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

A. Requesting Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time. You can initiate account deletion through:

  1. In-App: Navigating to Account Settings → Delete Account within the App.
  2. Web-Based Request: Submitting a request via our support email at [Click to reveal email or wait...].

B. Retention & Backup Policy

When you delete your account, your active profile, fitness logs, and personal data are immediately purged from our primary database. Encrypted database backups may retain residual data for up to 30 days before being permanently and irreversibly overwritten.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected personal data from a child without verified parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, providing in-app notifications or email notices.
